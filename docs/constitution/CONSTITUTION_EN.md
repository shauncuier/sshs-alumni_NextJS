## Preamble

Whereas the former students of Sabuj Shikshayatan Government High School, Sitakunda, Chattogram, founded in 1976, established "Praktan Sabujian Parishad" in 2015 with the aim of preserving mutual unity and goodwill and of working for the welfare of their beloved alma mater and its students, and the Association has carried on its activities without interruption since that time; whereas it is necessary to conduct the activities of the Association under a written, democratic, transparent and accountable framework, and to make the Association fit for registration under the Societies Registration Act, 1860; and whereas the Association's activities of member registration, receipt of money, vouchers and the giving of notices are being conducted through the Association's website, and it is therefore necessary to frame clear rules for these activities; now, therefore, the General Council of the Association, in a non-political, non-profit, non-communal and voluntary spirit, hereby adopts and enacts this Constitution.

## Part B: Rules and Regulations

(Rules and regulations of the Association under section 2 of the Societies Registration Act, 1860)

### Chapter 1: Preliminary

#### Article 1: Title, Commencement and Continuity

1.1 These Rules and Regulations, together with the Memorandum of Association in Part A, shall be called the "Praktan Sabujian Parishad Constitution".

1.2 The Association was founded in 2015 and has existed without interruption since that time.

1.3 This Constitution shall take effect from the date of its adoption by the General Meeting, and from that date the Association shall be reconstituted under this Constitution.

1.4 The adoption of this Constitution shall not impair the continuity of the Association. The activities, accounts and records from before the adoption of the Constitution shall be dealt with in accordance with the transitional provisions of Article 62.

1.5 The Association shall be registered as a society with the RJSC under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 [*].

#### Article 2: Definitions

2.1 Unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context, in this Constitution—

(a) "the Act" means the Societies Registration Act, 1860;

(b) "RJSC" means the Office of the Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms, where the Registrar under the Act performs his or her functions;

(c) "the Association" means Praktan Sabujian Parishad;

(d) "the School" means Sabuj Shikshayatan Government High School, Sitakunda, Chattogram;

(e) "former student" means any woman or man possessing the qualifications described in Article 10.1;

(f) "SSC batch" means the group identified by the year in which a former student passed the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) or equivalent examination from the School; and, in the case of a former student who did not pass the SSC from the School, the group of the year in which he or she would have sat the SSC examination had he or she continued studying at the School;

(g) "member" means a member of any class described in Article 11.1;

(h) "voting member" means a member described in Article 11.2;

(i) "General Council" means the General Council constituted under Article 18;

(j) "General Meeting" means an Annual General Meeting, a Special General Meeting or a Requisitioned General Meeting of the General Council;

(k) "Executive Committee" means the Executive Committee constituted under Article 19;

(l) "officer" means any office-bearing member of the Executive Committee;

(m) "Chief Patron" means the Chief Patron described in Article 22.1;

(n) "Advisory Council" means the Advisory Council constituted under Article 22;

(o) "Batch Representative Council" means the Batch Representative Council constituted under Article 21;

(p) "Election Commission" means the Election Commission constituted under Article 33 or Article 38;

(q) "election schedule" means the timetable of an election announced by the Election Commission;

(r) "Schedule" means any of Schedule 1 to Schedule 5 annexed to this Constitution;

(s) "clear days" means full days counted excluding the day on which the notice is deemed to have been given and the day of the meeting;

(t) "financial year" means the period from 1 January to 31 December [*];

(u) "Website" means the website operated in the name of the Association by the Executive Committee and declared by it to be official, currently www.exsabujian.org;

(v) "electronic notice" means a notice given by e-mail, short message (SMS) or through the notice board of the Website;

(w) "close relative" means a person's husband or wife, parents, children and brothers and sisters; and "members of the same family" means persons who are close relatives of each other;

(x) "Code of Conduct" means the code of conduct adopted by the Executive Committee under Article 51.

2.2 In this Constitution, the following designations shall denote the following roles, and the detailed duties of each office are described in Article 20:

(a) "President": the constitutional head of the Association and the chair of its meetings;

(b) "Vice-President": the officer who assists the President and discharges the President's duties in his or her absence;

(c) "General Secretary": the honorary chief executive of the Association;

(d) "Joint General Secretary": the officer who assists the General Secretary and discharges the General Secretary's duties in his or her absence;

(e) "Treasurer": the officer responsible for the finances, accounting and bank transactions of the Association;

(f) "Organising Secretary": the officer responsible for organisational activities and the expansion of membership;

(g) "Office Secretary": the officer responsible for minutes and office records;

(h) "Publicity and Publications Secretary": the officer responsible for publicity, the media and publications;

(i) "Information and Technology Secretary": the officer responsible for the Website, the security of the register of members and the electronic voting system;

(j) "Education, Scholarship and Library Secretary": the officer responsible for scholarships, educational programmes and the library;

(k) "Literary and Cultural Secretary": the officer responsible for literary and cultural activities;

(l) "Sports Secretary": the officer responsible for sports activities;

(m) "Social Welfare Secretary": the officer responsible for former student welfare and disaster relief activities;

(n) "Women's Affairs Secretary": the officer responsible for ensuring the participation of women former students in the activities of the Association;

(o) "Executive Member": a member of the Executive Committee elected without any specific office.

#### Article 3: Language

3.1 This Constitution has been drawn up in the Bangla language, and the Bangla text is the authoritative text.

3.2 An English translation of this Constitution shall be annexed to it, which is for information and assistance only.

3.3 In the event of any inconsistency between the Bangla text and the English translation, the Bangla text shall prevail.

#### Article 4: Nature

4.1 The Association is a non-political, non-profit, non-communal and voluntary organisation.

4.2 The Association is independent of the School, the government and any political party.

4.3 Notwithstanding anything contained in this Constitution, the Association shall have no authority over the administration of the School, the School Managing Committee or the funds of the School. The relationship of the Association with the School shall be governed by Article 49.

#### Article 5: Area of Operation

5.1 The area of operation of the Association is the whole of Bangladesh.

5.2 Members residing abroad may participate in the activities of the Association. Provided that, in the case of receiving money from abroad, Article 46 shall apply.

5.3 No branch of the Association may be formed without the written approval of the Executive Committee. An approved branch shall operate under the Executive Committee and in accordance with this Constitution.

#### Article 6: Logo, Seal and Name

6.1 The Association shall have an approved logo, the description of which is as follows: ________. A specimen of the approved logo shall be annexed to this Constitution.

6.2 The Association shall have a seal, which shall be used in accordance with the decisions of the Executive Committee.

6.3 The name, logo, seal or banner of the Association may not be used without a written decision of the Executive Committee.

6.4 Under no circumstances may the name, logo, seal or banner of the Association be used for party-political, commercial or personal purposes; nor may the name of the School be so used on behalf of the Association or by anyone acting as a member of the Association.

6.5 A violation of this Article shall be deemed a breach of discipline under Article 16.

### Chapter 2: Objects, Powers and Application of Income

#### Article 7: Detailed Objects

7.1 To carry out the objects described in Article A-3 of the Memorandum of Association, the Association shall undertake the following activities:

(a) Unity and communication: establishing unity, goodwill and a sense of fraternity among former students, maintaining contact on an SSC-batch basis and keeping the register of members up to date;

(b) Student welfare: providing scholarships, stipends and other welfare assistance to meritorious and financially needy students of the School;

(c) Education and careers: conducting mentoring, career guidance, seminars, workshops and educational programmes for students and young former students;

(d) Library and publications: supporting the library and reading room of the School, and producing souvenir publications, periodicals and other educational publications;

(e) Former student welfare: providing financial or other assistance to former students in distress;

(f) Disaster relief: conducting and taking part in relief activities in times of disaster;

(g) Preservation of history: preserving the history, heritage and memories of the School and organising commemorative events relating to them;

(h) Cooperation in School events: providing cooperation in the events and activities of the School in coordination with the School authorities;

(i) Reunion: organising a reunion of former students every five years.

7.2 In conducting these activities, the Association shall comply with Article 4 and Article 49.

#### Article 8: Powers

8.1 To carry out the objects of the Association, and subject to the law in force and this Constitution, the Association may exercise the following powers:

(a) opening and operating bank accounts and other financial accounts in the name of the Association in accordance with Article 41;

(b) raising funds;

(c) accepting sponsorship, grants and donations, subject to Article 45 and Article 46;

(d) acquiring, holding and managing immovable and movable property;

(e) creating separate funds for special purposes;

(f) forming sub-committees in accordance with Article 23;

(g) operating the Website and publishing;

(h) any other lawful act conducive to carrying out the objects of the Association.

#### Article 9: Application of Income

9.1 The income and property of the Association shall be used solely to carry out the objects of the Association.

9.2 No dividend, salary, allowance, honorarium or any other kind of financial benefit may be paid from the income or property of the Association to any member of the Association or to any member of the Executive Committee.

9.3 Personal expenses actually incurred on the work of the Association and supported by evidence may be reimbursed through vouchers in accordance with Article 43. Such reimbursement shall not be deemed a financial benefit under Article 9.2.

9.4 No salaried employee of the Association may be a member of the Executive Committee.

### Chapter 3: Membership

#### Article 10: Eligibility

10.1 Any of the following persons who has faith in the ideals, objects and Constitution of the Association shall be eligible for membership:

(a) a person who has passed the SSC or equivalent examination from the School; or

(b) a person who has studied at the School for at least one full academic year [*].

10.2 Every member shall be registered under his or her own SSC batch. A person eligible under Article 10.1(b) shall be registered under the batch with which he or she would have sat the SSC examination had he or she continued studying at the School.

10.3 An application for membership may be made through the Website or on the paper form prescribed in Schedule 2.

10.4 The Executive Committee shall approve applications for membership. Where necessary, the Executive Committee may ask the applicant for proof of eligibility.

#### Article 11: Classes of Membership

11.1 Membership of the Association is of the following five classes:

(a) Ordinary (Life) Member: a former student eligible under Article 10 who has paid the one-time membership fee prescribed in Schedule 1 and whose payment of the fee has been confirmed by the Association;

(b) Registered Member: a former student eligible under Article 10 who has registered on the Website but has not paid the membership fee; he or she may participate in the activities of the Association, but may not vote or stand as a candidate in an election;

(c) Honorary Member: a distinguished person elected by the Annual General Meeting (AGM) on the proposal of the Executive Committee; he or she shall have no voting rights;

(d) Associate Member: a teacher or employee currently serving at the School; he or she shall not be required to pay any fee and shall have no voting rights;

(e) Donor Member: a person who donates to the Association an amount greater than that prescribed in Schedule 1; he or she shall have no voting rights. Provided that, if a Donor Member is a former student, he or she shall also be deemed an Ordinary (Life) Member and, subject to Article 11.2, shall be a voting member.

11.2 An Ordinary (Life) Member who has attained the age of 18 years [*] is a voting member. Only voting members may vote at General Meetings, stand as candidates in elections, be counted towards the quorum and request a Requisitioned Meeting under Article 29.

11.3 An Ordinary (Life) Member under the age of 18 years shall become a voting member on the date of attaining the age of 18 years, without any additional application or fee.

#### Article 12: Fees

12.1 Life membership shall be acquired through payment of a one-time membership fee.

12.2 The Association shall not collect any annual subscription or renewal fee.

12.3 The amount of the membership fee shall be prescribed in Schedule 1. The Executive Committee shall propose Schedule 1 and the Annual General Meeting shall approve it.

12.4 If the amount of the membership fee is changed, the membership and status of those who have already paid the fee shall remain unaffected, and no additional amount may be demanded from them.

12.5 In cases of financial hardship, the Executive Committee may waive the membership fee of any applicant in whole or in part, in the manner described in Schedule 1. The decision to waive shall be recorded.

#### Article 13: Register of Members

13.1 The register of members maintained on the Website is the authoritative register of members of the Association. The register shall contain each member's name, SSC batch, contact details, national identity card number, photograph and details of payments.

13.2 The information in the register shall be used solely for the purposes of the Association. This information may never be sold or exchanged with any third party.

13.3 Only an officer who requires it for the performance of his or her duties may view the relevant information in the register.

13.4 Every member may view his or her own information in the register and request its correction.

13.5 The register shall be maintained electronically and may be produced in printed or exportable form for audit or legal requirements.

#### Article 14: Rights and Duties

14.1 Every voting member shall have the following rights:

(a) to vote at General Meetings and in elections;

(b) to stand as a candidate in elections in accordance with this Constitution;

(c) to request a Requisitioned Meeting under Article 29;

(d) to inspect the accounts of the Association under Article 47.

14.2 Every member may participate in the activities of the Association according to his or her class.

14.3 The duties of every member are:

(a) to abide by this Constitution, the Code of Conduct and the decisions of the General Council and the Executive Committee;

(b) to protect the dignity and interests of the Association;

(c) to keep his or her contact details in the register up to date.

#### Article 15: Termination of Membership

15.1 Membership shall terminate on any of the following grounds:

(a) death;

(b) submission of a written letter of resignation to the General Secretary, which shall take effect on the date of its receipt;

(c) expulsion under Article 16.

15.2 Membership shall remain suspended during the following periods:

(a) while the member stands convicted by a court of any offence involving moral turpitude;

(b) while the member is a salaried employee of the Association.

15.3 While membership is suspended, the member concerned may not vote, stand as a candidate in an election or continue to hold any office of the Association.

#### Article 16: Discipline

16.1 If any member acts contrary to this Constitution, the objects of the Association or the Code of Conduct, or if his or her conduct is detrimental to the dignity and interests of the Association, action may be taken against him or her under this Article.

16.2 Disciplinary action shall be taken in the following stages:

(a) Complaint: a specific complaint shall be submitted in writing to the General Secretary;

(b) Inquiry: the Executive Committee shall form a three-member inquiry committee, which shall investigate the complaint and submit a written report to the Executive Committee;

(c) Show-cause notice: the accused member shall be given a 7-day show-cause notice at the e-mail address or mobile number recorded in the register and by post;

(d) Hearing: the accused member shall be given an opportunity of a hearing to defend himself or herself.

16.3 After the above stages have been completed, the Executive Committee may, by a vote of two-thirds of its total members, suspend the membership of the accused member or expel him or her.

16.4 The member concerned may appeal to the next General Meeting within 30 days of being informed of the decision. The General Meeting shall decide the appeal by secret ballot.

16.5 The detailed procedure for discipline and appeals shall be prescribed in Schedule 5.

#### Article 17: Readmission

17.1 A person whose membership has terminated may apply to regain membership by written application to the President or the General Secretary. Readmission may be granted with the approval of two-thirds of the total members of the Executive Committee.

17.2 Provided that an expelled member may not apply for readmission before 2 years [*] have elapsed from the date of expulsion.

#### Article 20: Duties of Officers

20.1 President:

(a) shall act as the constitutional head of the Association;

(b) shall preside over General Meetings and meetings of the Executive Committee;

(c) shall, in the event of an equality of votes at any meeting, give the deciding (casting) vote;

(d) shall represent the Association and, in accordance with the decisions of the Executive Committee, sign agreements and deeds on behalf of the Association;

(e) shall supervise the implementation of the Constitution and the work of the officers;

(f) shall be a signatory to the bank accounts in accordance with Article 41.

20.2 Vice-President:

(a) shall assist the President in his or her work;

(b) in the absence of the President, one Vice-President, according to the order of seniority of SSC batch, shall preside over the meeting;

(c) if the office of President falls vacant, the most senior Vice-President shall discharge the duties of the President until the office is filled in accordance with Article 24.

20.3 General Secretary:

(a) shall, as the honorary chief executive of the Association, be accountable to the President and the Executive Committee;

(b) shall, in consultation with the President, convene General Meetings and meetings of the Executive Committee and issue notices;

(c) shall implement the decisions of the General Council and the Executive Committee;

(d) shall, with the assistance of the Office Secretary, ensure the safekeeping of all records of the Association;

(e) shall communicate and correspond on behalf of the Association with the government, the School authorities, donors and other institutions;

(f) shall present the annual report at the Annual General Meeting;

(g) in accordance with section 6 of the Act, the Association shall sue in his or her name, and suits may be brought against the Association in his or her name (Article 52);

(h) shall complete the necessary filings with the RJSC in accordance with Article 53;

(i) shall be a signatory to the bank accounts in accordance with Article 41.

20.4 Joint General Secretary:

(a) shall assist the General Secretary in his or her work;

(b) shall, in the absence of the General Secretary, discharge the duties of the General Secretary in the order determined by the Executive Committee;

(c) shall perform other duties assigned by the Executive Committee.

20.5 Treasurer:

(a) shall receive all moneys of the Association against serially numbered receipts;

(b) shall keep vouchers, bills and supporting documents in accordance with Article 43;

(c) shall maintain the cash book, ledger and other books of account in accordance with Article 48;

(d) shall operate the bank accounts jointly with one other signatory in accordance with Article 41;

(e) shall prepare the draft annual budget (Article 44);

(f) shall liaise with the auditor to complete the audit and shall present the audited accounts at the Annual General Meeting.

20.6 Organising Secretary:

(a) shall conduct the organisational activities of the Association;

(b) shall take initiatives to increase the number of members by contacting former students;

(c) shall coordinate the Executive Committee with the Batch Representative Council;

(d) shall place proposals for the formation of branches before the Executive Committee.

20.7 Office Secretary:

(a) shall carry out all office work in consultation with the General Secretary;

(b) shall prepare the minutes of all meetings and preserve them in accordance with Article 32;

(c) shall preserve the records, statistics and reports of the Association;

(d) shall assist in sending notices of meetings to members.

20.8 Publicity and Publications Secretary:

(a) shall publicise the objects and programmes of the Association;

(b) shall prepare and publish the publications of the Association;

(c) shall maintain contact with the media in accordance with the decisions of the Executive Committee;

(d) shall, in coordination with the Information and Technology Secretary, publish notices and information on the notice board of the Website.

20.9 Information and Technology Secretary:

(a) shall operate and maintain the Website of the Association;

(b) shall ensure the security of the register of members and the control of access to its information (Article 13);

(c) shall provide technical assistance to the Election Commission in operating the electronic voting system;

(d) shall ensure regular backups of the electronic records of the Association.

20.10 Education, Scholarship and Library Secretary:

(a) shall conduct scholarship and stipend activities;

(b) shall organise mentoring, career guidance and educational programmes;

(c) shall conduct activities relating to the library and reading room.

20.11 Literary and Cultural Secretary:

(a) shall organise literary and cultural events;

(b) shall assist the Publicity and Publications Secretary with literary publications.

20.12 Sports Secretary:

(a) shall organise and conduct all sports activities of the Association.

20.13 Social Welfare Secretary:

(a) shall conduct welfare activities for former students in distress;

(b) shall conduct relief activities in times of disaster;

(c) shall keep records of welfare activities.

20.14 Women's Affairs Secretary:

(a) shall ensure the participation of women former students in the activities of the Association;

(b) shall organise programmes for women former students and female students.

20.15 Executive Member:

(a) shall attend and vote at meetings of the Executive Committee;

(b) shall perform duties assigned by the Executive Committee.

20.16 Every officer shall act in accordance with the decisions of the Executive Committee, perform additional duties assigned by the Executive Committee and, at the end of the term, hand over charge in accordance with Article 39.

#### Article 21: Batch Representative Council

21.1 The Batch Representative Council [*] shall be formed with one representative from each SSC batch.

21.2 The voting members of each batch shall elect the representative of their own batch through the Website. The procedure for electing representatives and their term shall be prescribed in Schedule 3.

21.3 The Batch Representative Council is an advisory body. It shall have no executive power, and batch representatives may not vote in the Executive Committee.

21.4 The Batch Representative Council shall meet with the Executive Committee at least twice a year.

21.5 Every batch representative shall encourage the members of his or her own batch to participate in the events and activities of the Association and to pay the membership fee.

#### Article 22: Chief Patron and Advisory Council

22.1 The serving Headmaster of the School shall ex officio be the Chief Patron [*] of the Association. The Chief Patron shall have no voting rights.

22.2 The Chief Patron may, on invitation, attend General Meetings and meetings of the Executive Committee and give his or her opinion.

22.3 Within 1 month of assuming office, the Executive Committee shall nominate an Advisory Council of 7 to 11 members, consisting of an odd number of members.

22.4 The former Presidents of the Association shall, subject to their consent, be included in the Advisory Council.

22.5 The Advisory Council is an advisory body and shall have no voting rights in the Executive Committee. However, the Advisory Council shall exercise the powers described in Article 37, Article 38 and Article 56.

22.6 The Advisory Council shall remain in place for the term of the Executive Committee that nominated it. Provided that the existing Advisory Council shall continue to discharge its duties until the new Executive Committee assumes office and nominates a new Advisory Council.

#### Article 23: Sub-committees and Co-opted Coordinators

23.1 The Executive Committee may form any necessary sub-committee, including sub-committees on events, fundraising, mentoring, audit and finance.

23.2 The decision forming a sub-committee shall state its terms of reference, convener, members and term. Every sub-committee shall be accountable to the Executive Committee and may not take any decision beyond the powers delegated to it.

23.3 The Executive Committee may co-opt coordinators for specific tasks. A co-opted coordinator is not a member of the Executive Committee and shall have no voting rights.

#### Article 24: Vacancies, Absence and Resignation

24.1 Any member of the Executive Committee may resign in writing to the President, and the President may resign in writing to the General Secretary. The letter of resignation shall be placed before the next meeting of the Executive Committee and shall take effect on the date on which it is so placed.

24.2 If any office of the Executive Committee falls vacant, the Executive Committee shall, with the support of two-thirds of its total members, fill the office by co-opting a voting member eligible to be elected to that office. The co-opted member shall serve for the remainder of the term, and his or her co-option must be approved at the next General Meeting.

24.3 Provided that, if the office of President or General Secretary falls vacant, it may not be filled through co-option. Such an office must be filled by election at a Special General Meeting convened under Article 29 within 60 days of the office falling vacant. Until the office is filled, the duties of the President shall be discharged in accordance with Article 20.2(c) and the duties of the General Secretary in accordance with Article 20.4(b).

24.4 If any member of the Executive Committee is absent from 3 consecutive meetings without the prior permission of the President or the General Secretary, he or she shall be given a 7-day show-cause notice. If no satisfactory reply is received, his or her office shall become vacant by decision of the Executive Committee.

#### Article 25: No-confidence

25.1 To bring a no-confidence motion against the Executive Committee or any of its members, a written application stating the reasons and signed by at least one-fifth [*] of the total voting members must be submitted to the President or, if the motion is against the President, to the General Secretary.

25.2 On receipt of the application, a Requisitioned General Meeting must be convened under Article 29. The quorum under Article 30.1 must be present at the meeting, and for want of a quorum the meeting shall stand cancelled rather than adjourned.

25.3 The person against whom the motion has been brought shall be given an opportunity to speak at the meeting.

25.4 The no-confidence motion shall be passed by a vote of two-thirds of the members present and voting at the meeting.

25.5 If a no-confidence motion is passed, the vacancy must be filled within 60 days in accordance with Article 24, or, where necessary, an election must be held. If a no-confidence motion is passed against the entire Executive Committee, the Advisory Council shall form a Convening Committee and an Election Commission in the manner set out in Article 38.2, and an election shall be held within 60 days.

#### Article 26: Conflict of Interest

26.1 If any member of the Executive Committee or of any sub-committee has a direct or indirect personal interest in any matter under discussion, he or she shall declare it at the very beginning of the discussion.

26.2 The member declaring the interest shall refrain from the discussion of the matter concerned and shall not vote on that matter. The declaration shall be recorded in the minutes.

26.3 Except by a resolution adopted by a two-thirds vote of a General Meeting, no payment may be made or contract given to any officer, or to any enterprise owned by an officer or by his or her close relative. However, this sub-article shall not apply to the reimbursement of expenses under Article 9.3.

### Chapter 5: Meetings

#### Article 27: Meetings of the Executive Committee

27.1 Meetings of the Executive Committee shall be held at least once every 2 months, that is, at least 6 times a year.

27.2 The General Secretary, in consultation with the President, shall convene a meeting by 7 days' notice together with the agenda. In case of urgent need, an emergency meeting may be convened by 24 hours' notice.

27.3 The quorum of a meeting shall be constituted by the presence of one-third of the then total membership of the Executive Committee.

27.4 The President shall preside over meetings; in the President's absence, Article 20.2(b) shall apply.

27.5 Unless otherwise provided in this Constitution, decisions shall be taken by a majority of the votes of the members present and voting.

27.6 Where an urgent decision is required, a decision may be taken without a meeting by a recorded electronic vote with the support of a majority of the then total membership of the Executive Committee. Provided that decisions on the following matters may not be taken by electronic vote:

(a) the removal, suspension or expulsion of any member or office-bearer;

(b) the amendment of the Constitution or of any Schedule;

(c) financial matters exceeding the limits prescribed in Schedule 4.

27.7 Every decision taken by electronic vote shall be recorded in the minutes of the next meeting.

#### Article 28: Annual General Meeting

28.1 An Annual General Meeting shall be held once every year by 30 April [*]. Provided that the interval between two Annual General Meetings shall not exceed 15 months.

28.2 The Annual General Meeting shall be convened by at least 21 clear days' notice together with the agenda.

28.3 The business of the Annual General Meeting shall be:

(a) approval of the minutes of the previous general meeting;

(b) receiving the annual report of the General Secretary;

(c) receiving the audited accounts presented by the Treasurer;

(d) adopting the budget for the current financial year;

(e) appointment of the auditor;

(f) in every second year, the election of the Executive Committee in accordance with Chapter 6;

(g) other matters specified in the notice.

28.4 After the Annual General Meeting, the list of the Executive Committee shall be filed with the RJSC in accordance with Article 53.

#### Article 29: Special and Requisitioned General Meetings

29.1 The President, or one-third of the total membership of the Executive Committee, may convene a Special General Meeting by 14 days' notice.

29.2 A Requisitioned General Meeting shall be convened if one-third of the total voting members or 50 voting members, whichever is fewer [*], make a signed written request stating the purpose of the meeting. The request may be submitted through the website or on paper.

29.3 Within 15 days of receiving the request, the Executive Committee shall convene the Requisitioned Meeting and issue the notice.

29.4 If the Executive Committee does not convene the meeting within 15 days, the requisitioning members may themselves convene the Requisitioned Meeting by 7 days' notice.

29.5 Only the matters stated in the requisition shall be discussed at a Requisitioned Meeting.

#### Article 30: Quorum and Adjournment

30.1 The quorum of a general meeting shall be constituted by the presence of one-tenth of the total voting members or 50 voting members, whichever is fewer [*]. Members present by electronic means in accordance with Article 31.1 shall be counted towards the quorum.

30.2 If a quorum is not present within 30 minutes of the scheduled time, the meeting shall stand adjourned for 7 days, and the adjourned meeting shall be held at the same time and at the same place. The adjourned meeting shall be held with however many members are present, and only the agenda of the original meeting shall be discussed at the adjourned meeting.

30.3 Provided that a Requisitioned Meeting, a meeting on a motion of no confidence, a meeting to amend the Constitution (Articles 57 and 58) and a meeting on dissolution (Article 60) shall not be adjourned for want of a quorum, but shall be cancelled.

30.4 Proxies shall not be accepted in any vote other than votes under Articles 58, 60 and 61 [*]. The proxy form shall be prescribed in Schedule 2.

#### Article 31: Electronic Meetings and Notices

31.1 Members may attend and vote at general meetings by video, and members of the Executive Committee may attend and vote at meetings of the Executive Committee by video, provided that the member can hear the proceedings of the meeting in real time and can be heard in real time. Such a member shall be deemed to be present at the meeting.

31.2 All notices shall be sent to the e-mail address and mobile number recorded in the register and posted on the notice board of the website.

31.3 An electronic notice shall be deemed to have been given 48 hours after it is sent.

31.4 By registering an e-mail address or mobile number in the register, the member concerned shall be deemed to have consented to receiving notices electronically.

#### Article 32: Minutes

32.1 The minutes of every general meeting and of every meeting of the Executive Committee shall be kept electronically, and regular backups of them shall be maintained.

32.2 The minutes of each meeting shall be approved at the next meeting and shall be signed by the person presiding over the meeting that approves them.

32.3 All decisions taken at general meetings shall be published on the website.

### Chapter 6: Elections

#### Article 33: Election Commission

33.1 For conducting the election of the Executive Committee, an Election Commission shall be formed of 3 voting members in total: a Chief Election Commissioner and 2 Election Commissioners.

33.2 No member of the Election Commission may be a candidate in the election, a member of the Executive Committee or a member of the Appeal Board under Article 37.2.

33.3 The Executive Committee shall appoint the Election Commission at least 60 days before the expiry of its term and shall announce the appointment on the website.

33.4 The Election Commission shall independently conduct the entire election in accordance with this Constitution and Schedule 3. The Executive Committee and the Information and Technology Secretary shall provide the Commission with the necessary assistance.

33.5 If any office in the Election Commission falls vacant, the Executive Committee shall fill it in compliance with the conditions of this Article.

#### Article 34: Voters

34.1 Only those whose payment of the membership fee has been confirmed before the announcement of the election schedule, and who are voting members on the date of the announcement of the election schedule, shall be voters.

34.2 The Election Commission shall publish a draft voter list on the website. Objections regarding the list may be submitted for up to 7 days after the publication of the draft list.

34.3 After disposing of the objections, the Election Commission shall publish the final voter list with photographs. No person whose name is not on the final voter list may vote.

#### Article 35: Candidacy and Term Limits

35.1 Only voting members included in the final voter list may be candidates in the election.

35.2 A candidate must have been a voting member for at least 2 years [*] before submitting the nomination paper. Provided that this sub-article shall take effect from the second election held after the adoption of this Constitution [*].

35.3 In the case of a candidate for the office of President, at least 20 years [*], and in the case of a candidate for the office of General Secretary or Treasurer, at least 12 years [*], must have elapsed since the year of the candidate's SSC batch.

35.4 No person may hold the same office for more than 2 consecutive terms.

35.5 Articles 19.5 and 19.6 shall apply to candidacy.

#### Article 36: Nomination and Voting

36.1 The Election Commission shall publish the election schedule on the website and by electronic notice at least 30 days before voting.

36.2 A separate election shall be held for each office. The 8 Executive Members shall be elected collectively by the votes of all voters, and each voter may vote for not more than 8 candidates for Executive Member.

36.3 The nomination fee shall be prescribed in Schedule 3 and shall be set at a modest amount.

36.4 Voting shall be by secret ballot. Voters may vote online on the website through one-time password (OTP) verification, or at a polling station (polling booth) set up at the venue of the Annual General Meeting [*]. A voter may vote only once.

36.5 If there is only one valid candidate for an office, the Election Commission shall declare that candidate elected unopposed.

36.6 The Election Commission shall publish the results on the website within 24 hours of the close of voting.

36.7 The Election Commission shall inform the office-bearer responsible for filing with the RJSC under Article 53 of the election results in writing.

36.8 Detailed provisions on the election timetable, the OTP voting procedure, the polling station, the counting of votes and the time limits for objections shall be prescribed in Schedule 3.

#### Article 37: Election Disputes

37.1 Any objection relating to the election must be submitted in writing to the Election Commission within 48 hours of the decision or event concerned or, in the case of the results, of the publication of the results.

37.2 After the announcement of the election schedule, the Advisory Council shall form an Appeal Board of 3 members from among its own members. No candidate or close relative of a candidate may be a member of the Appeal Board.

37.3 A person aggrieved by a decision of the Election Commission may appeal to the Appeal Board within 3 days of being notified of the decision.

37.4 The decision of the Appeal Board shall be deemed final.

#### Article 38: Failure to Hold an Election

38.1 If an election is not held within 60 days after the expiry of the term of the Executive Committee, the outgoing Executive Committee shall stand dissolved.

38.2 In such a case, the Advisory Council shall form a Convening Committee of 5 members, including a Convener, and an Election Commission in compliance with the conditions of Article 33.

38.3 The election shall be held within 90 days of the formation of the Convening Committee.

38.4 The Convening Committee shall conduct the day-to-day activities of the Association until the elected Executive Committee assumes office, and shall assist the Election Commission in holding the election.

#### Article 39: Handover of Office

39.1 The new Executive Committee shall assume office within 14 days of the publication of the results.

39.2 The outgoing Executive Committee or the Convening Committee shall hand over to the new Executive Committee all documents, passwords, bank mandates, administrative rights to the website, seals, cash and other assets of the Association. Both parties shall sign a list of the items handed over.

39.3 The change of signatories at the bank must be completed within 21 days of the new Executive Committee assuming office.

39.4 The list of the Executive Committee must be filed with the RJSC in accordance with section 4 of the Act within 14 days of the Annual General Meeting (Article 53).

### Chapter 7: Financial Management

#### Article 40: Funds and Financial Year

**40.1** The Association shall have a General Fund. All money received from the following sources shall be deposited into this fund:

- (a) membership fees;
- (b) registration fees for events, reunions and other programmes;
- (c) donations and grants from members and well-wishers;
- (d) money received from sponsorship;
- (e) grants from government, semi-government or private institutions;
- (f) profit earned on bank accounts; and
- (g) any other source lawful under the laws in force.

**40.2** The money in the fund and all assets of the Association shall be used, in accordance with Article 9, solely to implement the aims and objectives set out in this Constitution.

**40.3** The financial year of the Association shall run from 1 January to 31 December of each year [*].

**40.4** Receipts and vouchers for each financial year shall begin with new serial numbers.

#### Article 41: Bank Accounts and Mobile Financial Service Accounts

**41.1** A current or savings account shall be opened in the name of the Association with any scheduled bank. Where necessary, more than one account may be opened by decision of the Executive Committee, but every account shall be in the name of the Association.

**41.2** The President, the General Secretary and the Treasurer shall be the signatories of the bank account. The account shall be operated by the joint signatures of any two of them, but the Treasurer shall always be one of the two signatories.

Provided that, if the office of Treasurer is vacant, the account shall be operated by the joint signatures of the President and the General Secretary.

**41.3** The signatories shall not be close relatives of one another. In this Article, "close relative" means spouse, parent, child and sibling.

**41.4** Signatories shall be appointed and changed by a written decision of the Executive Committee, and a certified copy of the decision shall be submitted to the bank. After a new Executive Committee assumes office, the signatories at the bank shall be changed within the time prescribed in Article 39.

**41.5** Any bKash, Nagad or other mobile financial service account, and any Bangla QR or other merchant account, shall be opened and operated only in the name of the Association. Money deposited in such accounts shall be transferred to the Association's bank account at the intervals prescribed in Schedule 4, but at least once every month [*], and every transfer shall be recorded in the books of account.

**41.6** No money of the Association may be received or kept in the personal bank account, personal mobile financial service account or personal mobile number of any member or office-bearer.

**41.7** All payments of the Association, other than cash expenditure permitted under Schedule 4, shall be made by cheque, by bank transfer or through a mobile financial service account in the name of the Association.

#### Article 42: Cash in Hand and Spending Powers

**42.1** The Treasurer may keep cash in hand for day-to-day expenses up to the maximum limit prescribed in Schedule 4, that is, Tk 10,000 (ten thousand taka) [*]. Cash in excess of this limit must be deposited into the bank account within 7 (seven) days [*] of its receipt.

**42.2** Expenditure shall be approved according to the tiers prescribed in Schedule 4:

- (a) each single item of expenditure included in the approved budget, up to Tk 5,000 (five thousand taka) [*], with the approval of the General Secretary or the President;
- (b) each single item of expenditure included in the approved budget that exceeds Tk 5,000 (five thousand taka), up to Tk 50,000 (fifty thousand taka) [*], by decision of a meeting of the Executive Committee;
- (c) each single item of expenditure exceeding Tk 50,000 (fifty thousand taka), or any expenditure of any amount outside the approved budget, by decision of two-thirds of the members present and voting at a meeting of the Executive Committee. Every such expenditure must be reported to the next general meeting.

**42.3** No expenditure may be split into more than one part for the purpose of bringing it within a lower approval tier.

**42.4** The amounts mentioned in this Article form part of Schedule 4 and may be changed in accordance with Article 59. If Schedule 4 is changed, the changed amounts shall apply.

#### Article 43: Receipts, Vouchers and Approval

**43.1** A serially numbered receipt (printed or electronic) shall be issued against every receipt of money by the Association.

**43.2** A voucher must be prepared for every payment or transfer, and the relevant bill, cash memo or supporting evidence (the original copy or a scanned electronic copy) shall be attached to the voucher.

**43.3** An office-bearer who prepares or records a voucher may not approve that voucher. Every voucher shall be approved by an authorised office-bearer other than the one who prepared it.

**43.4** No person may approve any payment in his or her own favour. In the case of payments involving a conflict of interest covered by Article 26, the office-bearer concerned shall likewise not take part in the approval.

**43.5** An expenditure or transfer shall be treated as part of the Association's accounts only after it has been approved.

**43.6** No accounting entry may be deleted. Where an entry is to be cancelled, it shall be marked as cancelled, with the reason for cancellation, the date and the name of the person cancelling it recorded, and, if necessary, a new correcting entry shall be made.

**43.7** Every electronic payment (bank transfer, online payment or payment through a mobile financial service) must be approved in accordance with this Article before it is made, and a written or electronic record of that approval must be kept.

**43.8** The provisions of this Article shall apply regardless of the method, software or website through which the accounts of the Association are kept.

#### Article 44: Budget

**44.1** The Treasurer shall prepare a draft budget of income and expenditure by head for each financial year. The Executive Committee shall approve the draft budget, and the Annual General Meeting shall finally adopt it.

**44.2** Until the budget for the new financial year is adopted at the Annual General Meeting, the Executive Committee may incur expenditure within the head-wise limits of the budget adopted for the previous financial year.

**44.3** If required during the financial year, the Executive Committee may approve a supplementary budget. The supplementary budget must be presented to the next general meeting for approval.

**44.4** A separate budget shall be prepared for each event or programme. After the event has ended, a statement comparing the actual income and expenditure with the budget must be presented at the next meeting of the Executive Committee.

#### Article 45: Reserved Funds, Sponsorship and Gifts to the School

**45.1** The following reserved funds of the Association shall be kept under separate account heads:

- (a) the Scholarship Fund;
- (b) the Welfare Fund; and
- (c) the Permanent Fund (Endowment).

The Executive Committee may, where necessary, establish any other reserved fund for a specific purpose.

**45.2** Where a donor makes a donation for a specific purpose, that money shall be spent only for that purpose. The money of any reserved fund may not be transferred to or used for any other head.

**45.3** The capital of the Permanent Fund may not be spent; only the profit earned on it may be spent.

Provided that, if the donor has set a different condition in writing, that condition shall apply.

**45.4** The following provisions shall apply to sponsorship:

- (a) the contribution of every sponsor shall be publicly acknowledged;
- (b) in return for sponsorship, no sponsor shall acquire any control over, or any right in respect of, the decisions, programmes or management of the Association;
- (c) sponsorship may not be accepted from any political party or from any of its wings or affiliated organisations [*]; and
- (d) the name or logo of a sponsor may be used in the publications or publicity of the Association only in accordance with a decision of the Executive Committee and consistently with Article 6.

**45.5** Money, goods or services may be given to the School as a gift or grant only:

- (a) on the basis of a written request from the Headmaster of the School;
- (b) by decision of the Executive Committee and within the limits prescribed in Schedule 4; and
- (c) subject to obtaining an acknowledgement of receipt from the School.

Details of every gift or grant given to the School shall be published on the Association's website.

#### Article 46: Foreign Donations

**46.1** The Association shall not accept any foreign donation until it is registered with the NGO Affairs Bureau under the Foreign Donations (Voluntary Activities) Regulation Act, 2016 and has obtained the necessary approval under that Act.

**46.2** In this Article, "foreign donation" means a foreign donation as defined in that Act, and shall also include any donation or assistance sent from abroad by a Bangladeshi citizen living abroad, including a member living abroad.

**46.3** Members living abroad shall pay membership fees, event fees or donations only in Bangladeshi taka and through a bank or mobile financial service channel within Bangladesh [*].

**46.4** If any money sent from abroad is deposited by mistake into any account of the Association, it may not be spent; that money shall be returned to the sender, and the matter shall be reported to the next meeting of the Executive Committee.

#### Article 47: Audit and Publication of Accounts

**47.1** The accounts for each financial year shall be audited by a chartered accountant or a firm of chartered accountants [*]. The Annual General Meeting shall appoint the external auditor and fix the auditor's remuneration.

**47.2** The same external auditor or audit firm shall not be appointed for more than 5 (five) consecutive years.

**47.3** No member of the Executive Committee, nor any close relative of such a member, may be the external auditor of the Association.

**47.4** The Annual General Meeting shall form an Internal Audit Committee consisting of 3 (three) voting members who are not members of the Executive Committee. This committee shall:

- (a) review the Association's books of account, receipts, vouchers and bank statements at least once every three months;
- (b) submit a report on each review to the Executive Committee; and
- (c) present an annual report to the Annual General Meeting.

**47.5** The external auditor and the Internal Audit Committee shall be entitled to see all accounts, documents and electronic records of the Association. The Treasurer and the General Secretary shall provide all necessary information and cooperation.

**47.6** The audited accounts shall be kept open for inspection by members at the Association's office and in the members' section of the website from at least 8 (eight) days before the Annual General Meeting. After being presented at the Annual General Meeting, the audited accounts shall be published on the Association's website.

#### Article 48: Books of Account and Records

**48.1** The Association shall maintain the following books of account and records:

- (a) cash book;
- (b) general ledger;
- (c) receipt and voucher files;
- (d) statements of bank accounts and mobile financial service accounts;
- (e) register of members;
- (f) minute book of meetings;
- (g) register of assets; and
- (h) budgets, annual reports and audit reports.

**48.2** These books of account and records may be kept electronically. Regular backups of electronic records must be kept, at least one copy of which shall be held in a location separate from the main place of storage. For audit, for filings with the Registrar or for legal requirements, all records shall be capable of being printed or exported in readable form.

**48.3** The General Secretary shall be responsible for the overall custody of the Association's records, the Treasurer for the financial books of account, and the Information and Technology Secretary for the backup of electronic records and the security of access to them. Access to the information in the register of members shall be controlled in accordance with Article 13.

**48.4** Any voting member may, on written application to the General Secretary, inspect the Association's books of account at a reasonable time. However, the personal information of other members shall not fall within the scope of this inspection.

### Chapter 8: Relationship with the School and Conduct

#### Article 49: Relationship with the School

**49.1** Every programme to be held on the School premises, or involving current students of the School, shall be organised in written coordination with the Headmaster of the School and with the Headmaster's written consent.

**49.2** The Association shall not interfere in any matter relating to the School's admissions, the appointment or transfer of teachers and staff, the School Managing Committee or the School's funds.

**49.3** Neither the Association nor any of its members shall claim to speak on behalf of the School or to represent the School.

#### Article 50: Safeguarding of Students

**50.1** The Executive Committee shall adopt a policy on the safeguarding of students and shall review it every year.

**50.2** The names of members or volunteers who will work with students in any programme (such as mentoring, scholarship interviews or events) must be approved by the School authorities, and the Executive Committee shall obtain character references for them.

**50.3** All contact with students shall take place in groups and under the supervision of a teacher of the School or an office-bearer of the Association assigned to that responsibility.

**50.4** Any incident or complaint relating to the safeguarding of students shall be recorded confidentially and securely, shall be reported immediately to the Headmaster of the School and, where required by law, shall be notified to the appropriate authority.

#### Article 51: Code of Conduct and Social Media

**51.1** The Executive Committee shall adopt a code of conduct for members as a policy, publish it on the website and review it every year. All members shall be bound to comply with this code of conduct.

**51.2** Only the website, social media pages, groups or accounts approved by the Executive Committee may publish statements on behalf of the Association. No person may operate any account, page or group in the name of the Association or of the School without approval.

**51.3** No person may collect any money or goods in the name of the Association or of the School without the written approval of the Executive Committee.

**51.4** The name, logo, seal or banner of the Association may not be used in contravention of the provisions of Article 6.

**51.5** Any complaint against a member of defamation, harassment, contravention of this Article or breach of the code of conduct shall be dealt with in accordance with Article 16 and Schedule 5.

### Chapter 9: Legal Matters

#### Article 52: Legal Proceedings

**52.1** In accordance with section 6 of the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Act No. XXI of 1860), the Association shall sue in the name of the General Secretary, and suits against the Association may be brought in the name of the General Secretary.

**52.2** The General Secretary shall not file, withdraw or compromise any suit on behalf of the Association without a decision of the Executive Committee.

**52.3** If the office of General Secretary is vacant, or the General Secretary is unable to perform the duties of the office, suits shall be filed and conducted in the name of the Joint General Secretary nominated by the Executive Committee as Acting General Secretary.

#### Article 53: Statutory Filings

**53.1** Within 14 (fourteen) days after each Annual General Meeting is held, a list of the names, addresses and occupations of the members of the Executive Committee shall be filed with the Registrar of the Department of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC).

**53.2** If there is any change in the name or address of the Association or in the list of members of the Executive Committee, and if this Constitution or any of its Schedules is amended, the Registrar shall be informed within 21 (twenty-one) days of the change.

**53.3** The General Secretary shall be responsible for all filings under this Article. A copy of every filing, and its acknowledgement of receipt, shall be kept in accordance with Article 48.

#### Article 54: Indemnity and Liability of Members

**54.1** If any member of the Executive Committee, or any member of the Advisory Council, the Election Commission or any sub-committee, personally incurs any liability or loss for any act done in good faith on behalf of the Association, that member shall be indemnified from the funds of the Association.

Provided that this indemnity shall not apply to any liability arising from wilful negligence, misconduct, fraud or breach of law.

**54.2** The personal liability of any member for any liability of the Association shall be limited to that member's unpaid membership fee, if any.

#### Article 55: Supremacy of Law

**55.1** Notwithstanding anything contained in this Constitution, the provisions of the Societies Registration Act, 1860 and of the other applicable laws and rules in force in Bangladesh shall prevail.

**55.2** If any provision of this Constitution is inconsistent with any law in force, only the inconsistent part shall be ineffective; the remaining provisions of the Constitution shall remain in force.

#### Article 56: Interpretation and Dispute Resolution

**56.1** The Executive Committee shall give the interpretation of any provision of this Constitution. Where the Constitution makes no provision on a matter, the Executive Committee shall take a decision consistent with the aims and objectives of the Association and shall report it to the next general meeting.

**56.2** Any voting member who disagrees with an interpretation given by the Executive Committee may refer the matter in writing to the Advisory Council. The opinion of the Advisory Council shall be binding until the next general meeting, and the decision of the general meeting shall be deemed final.

**56.3** In the case of disputes among members relating to the activities of the Association, or disputes between a member and the Association, an attempt must be made, before the dispute is brought before a court, to settle it by mediation through a mediator nominated by the Advisory Council.

### Chapter 10: Amendment and Dissolution

#### Article 57: Amendment of the Constitution

**57.1** A proposal to amend this Constitution may be moved by the Executive Committee or by a written application signed by at least 25 (twenty-five) voting members.

**57.2** The exact text of the proposed amendment must be sent to all members in accordance with Article 31, and published on the website, at least 30 (thirty) days before the Annual General Meeting or a Special General Meeting convened for this purpose.

**57.3** The amendment shall be adopted by the votes of two-thirds of the voting members present in person or online and voting at the meeting. No change to the proposal that goes beyond the subject matter of the proposal as circulated may be accepted at the meeting.

**57.4** A meeting to consider an amendment shall not be adjourned for want of a quorum; it shall be cancelled in accordance with Article 30.

**57.5** After an amendment has been adopted, the General Secretary may, without any change of substance, correct the serial numbering of articles and sub-articles, cross-references, spelling, punctuation and formatting, and shall ensure consistency between the Bangla and English texts. In the event of any inconsistency between the texts, the Bangla text shall prevail in accordance with Article 3.

**57.6** An amendment shall be filed with the Registrar within 21 (twenty-one) days of its adoption.

**57.7** A change of the name or objects of the Association shall be made in accordance with Article 58, changes to Schedules and policies in accordance with Article 59, and dissolution in accordance with Article 60.

#### Article 58: Change of Name or Objects

**58.1** A change, extension or abridgement of the name or of the aims and objectives of the Association shall be carried out in accordance with section 12 of the Societies Registration Act, 1860 by the following procedure:

- (a) the Executive Committee shall prepare a written or printed report on the proposal;
- (b) the report shall be sent to every member at least 10 (ten) days before a Special General Meeting convened for this purpose;
- (c) at that meeting, the proposal must be agreed to by the votes of three-fifths of the members, cast in person or by proxy; and
- (d) the proposal must be approved (confirmed) by the votes of three-fifths of the members present at a second Special General Meeting convened one month after the first meeting.

**58.2** A proxy must be in writing and signed by the member giving it; only another voting member may be appointed as proxy; and the proxy must be submitted to the General Secretary before the meeting begins.

**58.3** No meeting under this Article shall be adjourned for want of a quorum; it shall be cancelled.

**58.4** The change shall be filed with the Registrar within 21 (twenty-one) days of its approval at the second meeting.

#### Article 59: Schedules and Policies

**59.1** Schedule 1 (Fee Schedule), Schedule 3 (Election Rules) and Schedule 4 (Financial Limits) may be changed by a simple majority of the votes of the voting members present and voting at a general meeting. The text of the proposed change must be sent with the notice of the meeting.

**59.2** Schedule 2 (Application and Nomination Forms) and Schedule 5 (Disciplinary and Appeal Procedure) may be changed by decision of two-thirds of the members present and voting at a meeting of the Executive Committee.

**59.3** No change to Schedule 3 made after the election schedule of an election has been announced shall apply to that election. No change to Schedule 5 made after a disciplinary complaint has been filed shall apply to the disposal of that complaint.

**59.4** Every change to a Schedule shall be filed with the Registrar in accordance with Article 53 and published on the website.

**59.5** The Executive Committee shall adopt policies, including policies on the code of conduct, privacy, the safeguarding of students, social media and sponsorship, and other policies, shall publish them on the website and shall review them every year. If any policy is inconsistent with this Constitution, the provisions of the Constitution shall prevail.

#### Article 60: Dissolution

**60.1** A proposal for the dissolution of the Association may be considered only at a Special General Meeting convened for this purpose. Notice of the meeting, together with the text of the proposal and the reasons for it, must be sent to all members at least 30 (thirty) days before the meeting.

**60.2** The proposal shall be adopted if not less than three-fifths of all the voting members of the Association vote in favour of dissolution, in person or by proxy. Sub-article 58.2 shall apply to proxies.

**60.3** A meeting to consider dissolution shall not be adjourned for want of a quorum; it shall be cancelled.

**60.4** If the Government is a member of, a contributor to, or otherwise interested in the Association, the Association may not be dissolved without the consent of the Government.

**60.5** If the proposal for dissolution is adopted, the Executive Committee shall pay all debts and liabilities of the Association, have final accounts prepared and audited, and transfer the assets in accordance with this Constitution. Any dispute in this regard shall be settled by the principal civil court of the district in accordance with section 13 of the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

#### Article 61: Assets after Dissolution

**61.1** After all debts and liabilities have been paid, no remaining assets or money shall in any circumstances be distributed among or paid to the members.

**61.2** The remaining assets shall be given to another registered society having aims and objectives similar to those of the Association, in order of preference to a society that works for the welfare of the students of Sabuj Shikshayatan Government High School [*]. The recipient society shall be determined at the time of dissolution by the votes of not less than three-fifths of the members present in person or by proxy. If no such determination is made, the court referred to in sub-article 60.5 shall determine the recipient.

**61.3** The money and assets of reserved funds shall be given to a recipient that will use them for the original purpose of the fund and in accordance with the donor's conditions.

### Chapter 11: Transitional Provisions

#### Article 62: Transitional Provisions

**62.1** The Executive Committee in office at the time of the adoption of this Constitution shall exercise all the powers and perform all the duties conferred on the Executive Committee by this Constitution until the Executive Committee elected at the first election under this Constitution assumes office. The first election shall be held within 12 (twelve) months [*] of the adoption of this Constitution.

**62.2** Members whose membership fee was confirmed according to the records of the Association's website before the adoption of this Constitution shall become voting members under this Constitution without paying any fee again, but the other conditions of Article 11 shall continue to apply.

**62.3** All acts done, decisions taken, and accounts and records kept in good faith in the name or on behalf of the Association from its founding in 2015 until the adoption of this Constitution are hereby deemed to be approved (ratified).

**62.4** After the adoption of this Constitution, the money and assets of the Association held by any individual or in any personal account must, immediately after the Association's bank account is opened in accordance with Article 41, be deposited into that account or handed over to the Association.

**62.5** All written or unwritten rules, regulations and constitutions of the Association in force before the adoption of this Constitution are hereby repealed.

Provided that this repeal shall not affect the validity of any act or decision approved under sub-article 62.3.

## Schedule 3: Election Rules

*(See Articles 33 to 39 and sub-article 59.1. May be changed by a simple majority of votes at a general meeting.)*

**3.1 Election schedule:** The Election Commission shall announce the election schedule specifying the date and time of each stage, publish it on the website and send it to all members in accordance with Article 31. The schedule shall comply with the following timetable:

| Stage | Task | Time limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Formation of the Election Commission | At least 60 days before the expiry of the term of the Executive Committee (Article 33) |
| 2 | Announcement of the election schedule and publication of the draft voter list on the website | At least 30 days before polling day |
| 3 | Objections to, or applications for correction of, the draft voter list | 7 days after publication of the draft list |
| 4 | Disposal of objections and publication of the final voter list with photographs | Within 3 days of the end of the objection period [*] and before the submission of nomination papers begins |
| 5 | Collection and submission of nomination papers | At least 3 days [*] after publication of the final voter list |
| 6 | Scrutiny of nomination papers and publication of the list of valid candidates | Within 2 days [*] following the last day for submission of nomination papers |
| 7 | Objections against scrutiny | To the Election Commission within 48 hours of publication of the list of valid candidates; against the Commission's decision, to the Appeal Board within 3 days |
| 8 | Withdrawal of candidacy | At least 1 day [*] after the disposal of objections and appeals relating to scrutiny |
| 9 | Publication of the final list of candidates and a specimen ballot | At least 7 days before polling day [*] |
| 10 | Election campaign | From publication of the final list of candidates until 24 hours before voting begins [*] |
| 11 | Voting (online voting on the website and a polling station at the venue of the Annual General Meeting) | On the day of the Annual General Meeting, during the hours fixed under sub-article 3.6 |
| 12 | Counting of votes | Immediately after the close of voting |
| 13 | Publication of results | Within 24 hours of the close of voting |
| 14 | Objections against the results | To the Election Commission within 48 hours of publication of the results |
| 15 | Appeal against the Commission's decision | To the Appeal Board within 3 days of publication of the Commission's decision |
| 16 | Handover of office | Within 14 days of publication of the results (Article 39) |

**3.2 Voter list:** Only voting members whose membership fee was confirmed before the announcement of the election schedule shall be included in the voter list. The voter list shall contain each voter's name, membership number, SSC batch and photograph. Mobile numbers, e-mail addresses, postal addresses and National Identity Card numbers shall not be shown in the list published on the website. Any member may, within the time limit for Stage 3, lodge a written objection concerning his or her own entry or any other entry; applications to correct a registered mobile number or e-mail address must also be made within this period.

**3.3 Nomination:**

- (a) A separate election shall be held for each office. The 8 (eight) Executive Members shall be elected collectively by the votes of all voters.
- (b) A candidate may submit a nomination paper for only one office.
- (c) Nomination papers may be submitted on paper, in the form set out in sub-article 2.2 of Schedule 2, or through the website.
- (d) The nomination fee for each office is Tk ________. The nomination fee shall be set at such a modest amount that no voting member is prevented from standing as a candidate for financial reasons. The nomination fee shall be credited to the General Fund of the Association and is not refundable.

**3.4 Scrutiny and withdrawal:** The Election Commission shall scrutinise each nomination paper in accordance with Article 35 and this Schedule, and shall give its reasons in writing if any nomination paper is rejected. A candidate may withdraw his or her candidacy by written application to the Commission within the time limit for Stage 8.

**3.5 Elected unopposed:** If, at the end of the withdrawal period, the number of valid candidates for an office is equal to or less than the number of seats for that office, the Commission shall declare that candidate or those candidates elected unopposed. If there is no valid candidate for an office, that office shall remain vacant and shall be filled in accordance with Article 24.

**3.6 Online voting:**

- (a) Online voting shall run from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. on the day of the Annual General Meeting or, if the Commission fixes different hours in the schedule, during those hours [*]. Voting at the polling station shall also close at the same time.
- (b) The voter shall log in to the Association's website and confirm his or her identity with a one-time password (OTP) sent to the mobile number or e-mail address registered in the voter list.
- (c) Each voter may vote only once. Once submitted, a vote may not be changed or withdrawn.
- (d) Votes shall be secret. The system shall store the record of who has voted separately from the content of the votes, and neither the Election Commission, nor the Executive Committee, nor any person with technical responsibilities shall be able to know which voter voted for whom.
- (e) While voting is in progress, no person may see the results or any partial tally of the votes received.
- (f) Before voting, the Election Commission shall test the online voting system and certify in writing that the system meets the conditions of this Schedule [*]. For this purpose the Commission may appoint technical assistants who are not candidates.
- (g) If the Information and Technology Secretary or any person with technical responsibilities is a candidate, that person shall not have administrative access to the voting system during the election.
- (h) If voting is disrupted by a technical fault, the Commission may decide to extend the online voting time or to hold a fresh poll, and shall immediately publish that decision on the website and inform all voters of it.

**3.7 Polling station:**

- (a) There shall be a polling station at the venue of the Annual General Meeting, at which voting shall be by secret ballot.
- (b) At the polling station, the voter's identity shall be verified against the final voter list with photographs.
- (c) Before a ballot is issued, the system shall be checked to establish whether the voter has voted online. A person who has voted online may not vote at the polling station. A voter who receives a ballot at the polling station shall immediately be marked in the system as "voted".
- (d) Each candidate may appoint one polling agent at the polling station.

**3.8 Counting of votes and results:**

- (a) After the close of voting, the polling station ballots shall be counted in the presence of the candidates or their agents, and the results of the online vote shall be obtained from the system and added to the results of the polling station.
- (b) For each office, the candidate receiving the highest number of votes shall be elected; for the office of Executive Member, the 8 (eight) candidates receiving the highest numbers of votes shall be elected.
- (c) If two or more candidates receive an equal number of votes, the result shall be decided by lot in the presence of the candidates or their agents [*].
- (d) All members of the Election Commission shall sign the office-wise statement of results. The Commission shall publish the results on the website within 24 hours of the close of voting and shall inform the General Secretary of the results in writing for the filing under Article 53.
- (e) The Commission shall preserve the ballots, the statements of results and the records of the online voting system until all objections and appeals have been disposed of.

**3.9 Objections and appeals:**

- (a) Any candidate may, within 48 hours of the publication of the results, lodge a written objection with the Election Commission stating the grounds. The Commission shall give a written decision within 3 days [*] of receiving the objection.
- (b) A candidate aggrieved by the Commission's decision may appeal to the Appeal Board within 3 days of the publication of the decision.
- (c) After the announcement of the election schedule, the Advisory Council shall form an Appeal Board of 3 (three) members from among its own members (Article 37.2). No candidate or close relative of a candidate may be a member of the Appeal Board.
- (d) The Appeal Board shall give its decision within 7 days [*] of receiving the appeal, and the decision of the Appeal Board shall be deemed final.
- (e) This sub-article shall also apply to objections and appeals relating to scrutiny, but the time limits shall be those set out in Stage 7 of sub-article 3.1.

**3.10 Electoral conduct:** No candidate or supporter of a candidate may use the funds, property, information from the register of members or logo of the Association in campaigning. The Election Commission may issue an electoral code of conduct consistent with this Schedule.

## Schedule 5: Disciplinary and Appeal Procedure

*(See Articles 16, 17 and 51 and sub-article 59.2. May be changed by decision of two-thirds of the Executive Committee.)*

**5.1 Complaint:** A complaint against a member of conduct contrary to the Constitution, the code of conduct or the interests of the Association must be submitted to the General Secretary in specific terms (with the facts, dates and evidence), in writing and signed. If the complaint is against the General Secretary, it must be submitted to the President. Anonymous complaints shall not be accepted.

**5.2 Preliminary consideration:** The Executive Committee shall consider the complaint at its next meeting after receiving it. If the complaint appears to be outside the scope of this Constitution or to be baseless, it may be dismissed with the reasons recorded; otherwise, an inquiry committee shall be formed.

**5.3 Inquiry committee:** The Executive Committee shall form an inquiry committee of 3 (three) members. The complainant, the accused, any close relative of either of them, or any person with an interest in the matter may not be a member of the inquiry committee. The inquiry committee shall submit a written report to the Executive Committee within 30 days [*] of its formation.

**5.4 Show-cause notice:** If the inquiry report finds the complaint to be true on the face of it, the General Secretary shall serve on the accused member a show-cause notice together with particulars of the complaint and a summary of the evidence. The notice shall be sent by e-mail or SMS and by post to the address recorded in the register of members. The accused member may submit a written reply within 7 (seven) days from the date on which the notice is deemed to have been given (Article 31).

**5.5 Hearing:** After receiving the reply, or on the expiry of the time for a reply, the Executive Committee shall give the accused member an opportunity to be heard in person or by video in his or her own defence. The accused member may be accompanied at the hearing by one voting member. The accused must be informed of the date of the hearing at least 7 (seven) days in advance [*].

**5.6 Decision:**

- (a) After the hearing, the Executive Committee may, by decision of two-thirds of the members present and voting at the meeting, impose any of the following penalties: (1) a written warning; (2) suspension of membership for a specified period; or (3) expulsion.
- (b) The complainant and the accused, if they are members of the Executive Committee, may not vote on this decision.
- (c) The decision and the reasons for it must be communicated in writing to the accused member within 7 days [*] of the decision being taken.

**5.7 Appeal:**

- (a) A penalised member may submit a written appeal to the General Secretary within 30 (thirty) days of receiving the decision. The appeal shall be included in the agenda of the next general meeting.
- (b) At the general meeting, after both the appellant and the Executive Committee have been heard, the appeal shall be decided by a simple majority of the votes, cast by secret ballot, of the voting members present and voting at the meeting.
- (c) The decision of the Executive Committee shall remain in force until the appeal has been decided.
- (d) The decision of the general meeting shall be deemed final within the Association.

**5.8 Readmission:** An expelled person may apply in writing for readmission after 2 (two) years [*] have elapsed from the date of expulsion. The application may be granted with the approval of two-thirds of the members present and voting at a meeting of the Executive Committee (Article 17).

**5.9 Confidentiality:** All records of disciplinary proceedings shall be kept confidentially and securely, and only the persons assigned responsibilities under this Schedule may see them. No details of the proceedings other than the final decision shall be published.
