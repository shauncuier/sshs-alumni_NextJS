The Mission

The Problems We Solved

Transforming Fragmented Records into an Empowered, Transparent Alumni Network

❌ Old Fragmented Challenges

  • • Alumni scattered across private Facebook groups without an authoritative database.
  • • Manual cash collection and spreadsheet reconciliation for reunions and school donations.
  • • Paper ticket forgery and gate congestion during large school reunions.
  • • No formal avenue for recent graduates to seek career guidance from accomplished alumni.

✅ New SSGHS Platform Solution

  • • Single Source of Truth: Centralized database with verified membership badges and privacy.
  • • Local Payments: Instant bKash, Nagad, and Cards checkout with digital audit receipts.
  • • Smart ID Pass: 3D digital card with HMAC-SHA256 encrypted QR for 1-second gate verification.
  • • Career & Mentorship: Alumni job board + 1-on-1 BCS/Corporate mentorship matching engine.
