# Why register online + privacy for women — script (draft v1)

About 65 s. Bangla voiceover (voice iLBn9rrLQfwXBfBvaJGt), English subtitles. Animated
icons and split-screen comparisons on the brand green/gold; no real member's photo.

Facts checked in the code:
- Women's photos are hidden by default (lib/members/gender.ts photoIsPublic): others and
  visitors see a plain silhouette; only she and the admins see the real photo, and the photo's
  web address stops working for anyone else (app/api/media/avatars, lib/members/photo-privacy.ts).
- She can show it later in Settings → Profile Photo (app/settings/page.tsx).
- Phone number and email are hidden by default for everyone (isPhonePublic / isEmailPublic
  default false; "Hidden (administrators only)").
- Proof of study (SSC certificate etc.) is seen by the committee only.
- Joining via /jubilee makes you a member; ticket QR on the dashboard, scanned at the gate.
